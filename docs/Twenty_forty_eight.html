game.js is a module to model and play "2048" and related games. It knows nothing about the browser: boards are plain arrays of rows.

(static) mutate_tiles(board, randomopt) → {Object}

Source:
  • game.js, line 327

Randomly shifts tiles to the value one step above or below their own. A tile of value v shifts with probability 1 / v, so low tiles change often and the highest tile rarely. A tile never goes below the smallest new tile value or above rules.win_tile.
